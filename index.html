AutoApply Assistant

A Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that autofills job applications and drafts tailored resume summaries and cover letters with Claude — without ever auto-submitting or guessing on sensitive questions.

What it does

  • Autofills contact/profile fields and uploads your resume file across LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and other career portals via a generic fallback adapter.
  • Generates a tailored professional summary, bullet points, and cover letter from your own master resume using the Anthropic Messages API, called directly from your browser with your own API key.
  • Automatically continues the fill flow across new tabs and cross-domain redirects (e.g. LinkedIn → a company's Workday instance).

What it will never do

  • Click Submit or Apply for you — the button is only highlighted in green; final submission is always manual.
  • Guess on salary, EEO, or legal/work-authorization questions — those fields are highlighted in amber for you to answer.
  • Invent resume content not already present in your pasted master resume.
  • Send your resume, job descriptions, or API key anywhere except directly to api.anthropic.com from your own browser.
